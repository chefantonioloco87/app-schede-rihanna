Alimentazione

Nutrition advice May-June organizzata per giorni di allenamento e giorni off.

Workout Day - Moderate

Totale giornaliero: 105 g proteine | 180-190 g carbo | 50 g grassi | circa 1900 kcal

Breakfast - circa 400 kcal

Option 1: 200 ml milk, 50 g oats, 1 banana, 10 g peanut butter.
Option 2: 170 g Greek yogurt, 45 g cereal, 1 fruit.
Option 3: 2 eggs, 50 g bread, 1 apple.

Lunch - circa 500 kcal

Option 1: 140 g chicken, 70 g rice raw, 10 g olive oil, vegetables.
Option 2: 150 g tuna, 220 g potatoes, vegetables.
Option 3: 2 eggs, 120 g egg whites, 60 g bread.

Pre-Workout - circa 220 kcal

Option 1: 170 g Greek yogurt + banana.
Option 2: 60 g bread + 60 g turkey slices.
Option 3: 3 rice cakes + 15 g nuts.

Dinner / Post-Workout - circa 480 kcal

Option 1: 150 g white fish + 220 g potatoes + vegetables + 10 g olive oil.
Option 2: 150 g chicken + 60 g bread + vegetables.
Option 3: 2 eggs + 150 g egg whites + 50 g rice.

Day Off - Low

Totale giornaliero: 100 g proteine | 135 g carbo | 50 g grassi | circa 1225 kcal

Breakfast - circa 350 kcal

Option 1: 200 ml milk + 45 g oats + 10 g almonds.
Option 2: 170 g Greek yogurt + 40 g cereal.
Option 3: 2 eggs + 40 g bread.

Lunch - circa 450 kcal

Option 1: 140 g chicken + 60 g rice + vegetables + 10 g olive oil.
Option 2: 150 g tuna + salad + 50 g bread.
Option 3: 150 g legumes + 50 g bread.

Snack - circa 200 kcal

Option 1: Greek yogurt + fruit.
Option 2: 2 rice cakes + peanut butter.
Option 3: 40 g turkey slices + fruit.

Dinner - circa 450 kcal

Option 1: 150 g salmon + vegetables.
Option 2: 150 g eggs/egg whites + vegetables + 10 g olive oil.
Option 3: 150 g white fish + salad + olive oil.

Extra Notes

Water: 2-2.5 L daily.
Sleep: 8+ hours.
1 free meal weekly is fine.
Focus on progressive overload in training.
Whey protein optional for convenience only.

Best Low-Cost Protein Sources

Eggs, chicken, tuna, Greek yogurt, milk, turkey slices, legumes.
